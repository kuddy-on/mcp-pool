# Code of conduct

MCPPool contributors are expected to collaborate respectfully and in good faith.

Welcome people regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint. Be specific and
constructive in technical disagreement, assume good intent, and focus criticism on the work.
Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory language, doxxing, and sustained
disruption are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the repository's security
contact. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when needed to protect the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical, with attention to the safety of the reporter.
